# 🌍 Code of Conduct

**VeltrixOS is a place for people who like fast, lean, well-built systems. Be kind and be constructive.**

</div>

---

## 🎯 Our pledge

We want everyone to take part in VeltrixOS free from harassment, regardless of age, background, experience level, nationality, gender, religion or any other characteristic. We pledge to act in ways that keep the community open, welcoming and healthy.

## ✅ Expected behavior

- Be respectful and patient, especially with beginners.
- Give and accept constructive feedback. Criticize code, not people.
- Assume good intent and ask for clarification before reacting.
- Credit other people's work.

## ❌ Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, trolling or personal attacks.
- Discriminatory jokes or language.
- Publishing other people's private information.
- Spam, or repeatedly pushing a request that was already declined.
- Anything else that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## 📏 Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all project spaces: issues, pull requests, discussions and any place where someone represents the project.

## 🔨 Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close or lock threads, and ban people who break these rules. Decisions are made fairly and with the context in mind.

> [!NOTE]
> To report a problem, use the repository's private reporting form: **Security** tab → **Report a vulnerability**, and state that it is a conduct report. Only the maintainers can see it.
